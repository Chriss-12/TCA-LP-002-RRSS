# Style lock: TCA La Paz social hub

Established: 2026-11-06. Source: supplied TCA logo and Linktree layout reference.

## Palette

- Background: `#F8F5F7`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` at 84 to 90 percent opacity
- Primary: `#75224F`
- Accent: `#A93B78`
- Text primary: `#201820`, contrast 15.99:1 on background
- Text muted: `#5F5360`, contrast 6.70:1 on background
- Button label: white, contrast 10.01:1 on primary
- Dark mode: not needed for this single-mode project

## Color contract

- Text-safe: primary, accent, text primary and text muted on background or white surfaces
- UI-safe: accent against light gray borders and all network icon colors against white
- Decorative only: pale borders against background and translucent ambient gradients

## Typography and shape

- Brand typography: preserved inside the official logo asset
- Interface: Segoe UI Variable, Aptos or Segoe UI
- Major radius: 32px
- Link radius: 22px
- Spacing: 4px base with 12px, 16px, 24px and 32px primary steps

## Direction

One centered link hub with a compact brand header and four large social rows. The signature is an autonomous burgundy, rose and blue-gray ambient gradient.

## Assets and motion

- Anchor asset: `src/assets/tca-la-paz-logo.png`
- Icons: one `react-icons` family for social and utility symbols
- Motion: 120 to 240ms interaction feedback, one 240ms staggered entrance and slow ambient drift
- Reduced motion: remove entrances and reduce transitions to immediate feedback

## Do not

- Do not invent achievements, metrics or testimonials.
- Do not use gradient text.
- Do not require gestures for navigation.
- Do not change decorative gradients in response to taps or pointer movement.
- Do not replace the TCA burgundy with a generic purple palette.
